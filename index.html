2,000 sq.ft. G+1 architectural plan with double-height living atrium. 100% compliant with PMRDA UDCPR Table 6D setbacks and Vedic 32-pada grid (Pushpadanta West entry, Agni kitchen, Nairutya master suite, Ishanya puja).

📈 Valuation & Growth Potential Fair Value

Developer quote of ₹ 1.15 Cr inclusive of taxes (₹ 5,950/sq.ft.) is fair for gated township infra. Proximity to Paud Road 6-laning (1.6 km) and Bhugaon Bypass (1.4 km) drives projected 5-year capital appreciation to ₹ 1.85 Cr – ₹ 2.10 Cr.
