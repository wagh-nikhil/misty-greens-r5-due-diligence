5. Hilly Terrain Engineering & Sub-Structure Specifics (Bhugaon Basalt Formation) Deccan Basalt Strata

Plot R5-180 sits in the Western Ghats Deccan volcanic trap at an elevation of ~610 meters above MSL. The site exhibits hard basalt rock underlying 0.5m of murrum topsoil, with a gentle 1.2-meter natural slope from West (road level) descending toward East (Amenity EA 3 buffer).

1. Pneumatic Breaker Rock Excavation:
Standard JCB bucket excavation will refuse at 0.6m depth. Foundation requires tractor-mounted pneumatic rock breakers (chiseling) to anchor 12 isolated column footings into compact amygdaloidal basalt at 1.8m depth.
2. Plinth Leveling & UCR Retaining Wall:
To create a flat, flood-proof ground plinth (+0.6m above road crown), an Uncoursed Rubble (UCR) stone masonry retaining wall (1.2m to 1.5m height) is mandated along the eastern boundary, backfilled with gravel and boulder weep holes.
3. Anti-Termite & Damp Proofing Course (DPC):
Because the plot borders natural forested green buffers, heavy-duty chlorpyrifos chemical soil treatment is required under the plinth, plus an elastomeric polymer waterproofing coat on the exterior plinth beam faces.
4. Earthquake & Wind Resistance:
Designed for Pune Seismic Zone III (IS 1893:2016) using M25 grade concrete and Fe550D TMT bars with ductile detailing to withstand hilly gust wind pressures (basic wind speed 39 m/s).
