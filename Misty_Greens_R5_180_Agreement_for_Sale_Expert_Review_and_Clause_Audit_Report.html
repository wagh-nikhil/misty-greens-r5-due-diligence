Profit-Making Contract: The agreement legally mandates a 10% markup profit margin for the promoter's maintenance agency on top of all operating costs and overheads!

Open-Ended Liability: If the promoter under-budgets operational expenses, plot owners must pay retroactive ad-hoc supplementary bills.

Severance of Lifelines: TMA holds the contractual power to shut off power, piped water, and sewer drainage lines to your villa upon 30 days default.

4. The 10 Major "Catches" & Red Flags (Expert Review) Forensic Legal Scrutiny
CATCH #1: Active Mortgage in Favor of Vistra ITCL (India) Ltd. Clause 7.1 & Clause 22.2
Verbatim Clause Extract:
"7.1 ...the said project land is free from encumbrances except the Mortgage deed dated 23.07.2021 bearing no. 10530/2021, in favour of Vistra ITCL (India) Ltd... Hence the Owner/Promoter if specifically required by the Purchaser may obtain requisite NOC of Vistra ITCL (India) Ltd..."
"22.2 ...the encumbrances/charges of Lender of the Owner/Promoter is on the said 'Kaleidoscope R5 - Misty Greens - phase 3-B' Project Land... As required by the Lender, the Purchaser/Allottee hereby agree, confirm and express his permission to any assignment by the Owner/Promoter in favour of the Lender or any third party nominated by it or the exercise of any takeover / step in rights of the Lender..."
The Legal Trap: While marketing materials claim the land is encumbrance-free, the agreement acknowledges an active commercial mortgage with Vistra ITCL. If the promoter defaults on its corporate debt, the lender has contractual "step-in" rights. Crucially, the promoter will only secure an NOC if the buyer specifically demands it in writing.
Required Remedy: Make the execution of the Agreement conditional upon the promoter furnishing an unconditional, plot-specific No Objection Certificate (NOC) and Release Letter from Vistra ITCL (India) Ltd. specifically releasing Plot R5-180 from the charge.
CATCH #2: ₹ 1,00,000/- Transfer Ransom & 100% Pre-Payment on Resale Clause 16.1
Verbatim Clause Extract:
"16.1 ...the Owner/Promoter on prior written request... shall issue in his favor necessary No Objection Certificate... provided, i) the Purchaser pays entire consideration amount to the Owner/Promoter before selling the said Plot, ii) the Purchaser pays to the Owner/Promoter transfer charges of Rs. 1,00,000/-..."
The Legal Trap: If your life circumstances change and you wish to resell or assign Plot R5-180 during the construction cycle, you cannot simply assign your rights. You must pay 100% of the plot cost upfront to the developer and hand over a non-refundable ₹ 1,00,000/- fee for a simple NOC. Under MahaRERA rulings, unreasonable transfer fees are illegal.
Required Remedy: Negotiate deletion of the ₹ 1 Lakh fee or reduce it to a nominal administrative charge (max ₹ 10,000/-) and delete the requirement to pay 100% consideration before assignment if construction is ongoing.
CATCH #3: 6-Month Delayed Refund & Liquidated Damages on Exit Clause 21.1.2 & 21.1.3
Verbatim Clause Extract:
"21.1.3 For whatsoever reason if the Allottee/s herein, without any default or breach on his/her/their part, desire to terminate this agreement... Promoter deducts (i) an amount of Rs. 1,00,000/- towards liquidated damages in addition to interest, (ii) stamp duty, registration, extra work, (iii) Service Tax, VAT, GST... within a period of six months..."
The Legal Trap: If you cancel without default, the promoter gets 6 full months to return your money without paying interest during this holding period, while simultaneously deducting ₹ 1 Lakh plus non-recoverable taxes. Under RERA Section 18, refunds must be processed within 45 to 60 days.
Required Remedy: Insist that refund timelines be aligned with MahaRERA Rule 18 (refund within 45 days) and cap administrative deductions.
CATCH #4: Front-Loaded Payment Milestones (Payment on "Start" of Works) Annexure "K" (Payment Schedule)
Payment Milestone Schedule:
Booking Advance: 10% At Agreement Execution: 20% Completion of Fencing: 9%
Start of Internal Road Work: 20% Start of Utility Work: 20% At Offer of Possession: 20%
The Legal Trap: Notice the critical phrasing: 20% is triggered upon "START of internal road work", and another 20% on "START of utility work". By the time a contractor merely dumps gravel or digs a trench, the developer demands 79% of the total consideration! If the developer halts work after "starting", 79% of your capital is trapped.
Required Remedy: Amend milestone triggers to "Completion of asphalt/paved internal roads fronting the plot" and "Completion of electrical cabling, water, and sewage lines to plot plinth".
CATCH #5: Promoter Retains Global FSI, TDR & All Open Spaces Clause 1.4, Clause 8.1, Clause 8.5
Verbatim Clause Extract:
"1.4 'Township Global FSI' Shall mean the right to claim, utilize and consume entire Floor Space Index (FSI/TDR)... on global basis... excluding the total FSI belonging to the said Project."
"8.5 The Overall Township Amenities, green spaces, public utility spaces, open spaces, gardens, shall vest in the Owner/Promoter or its assigns, and it shall have right to allot, sell, transfer, convey or otherwise deal with the same..."
The Legal Trap: The promoter explicitly keeps all unconsumed and future FSI/TDR generated across the 47-hectare land. Furthermore, Clause 8.5 states that township gardens and green spaces vest permanently with the promoter to "allot, sell, transfer, or convey" to third parties.
Required Remedy: Ensure that the internal sector open spaces (such as Amenity EA 3 behind Plot 180) are contractually locked as non-buildable green buffer zones that cannot be converted into high-density commercial or tower developments.
CATCH #6: Right to Grant Road Easements to External Third-Party Projects Clause 8.8 & Clause 10.1.3
Verbatim Clause Extract:
"8.8 ...the Owner/Promoter shall be absolutely entitled to use or permit to be used, the artery roads and/ or the internal roads... as an access to the other lands or projects to be developed by the Owner/Promoter or any other promoter, land owner in the vicinity..."
"10.1.3 The Owner/Promoter has specifically disclosed... that Owner/Promoter has given easement rights... to Sarang Kale. In future also Owner/Promoter shall have rights to give such easement rights with or without consideration to adjoining land owners..."
The Legal Trap: The promoter explicitly reserves the right to allow outside builders, adjoining village plot developers, and neighboring land owners to use Misty Greens' internal roads. This dilutes the "gated private community" security and increases heavy construction vehicular traffic past your plot.
Required Remedy: Seek written confirmation that internal 9-meter roads in Sector R5 will remain restricted to Misty Greens residents and not be turned into thoroughfare access for external village developments.
CATCH #7: Indefinitely Deferred Common Area Conveyance Clause 6.2, 6.3 & Clause 10.1.2
The Legal Trap: Under Section 17 of MahaRERA, the promoter must convey common areas to the association of allottees within 3 months of receiving the Occupancy Certificate. In this Agreement, conveyance of the common roads, clubhouse, and township infrastructure is pushed back until the entire 47-hectare township is fully developed. Because township completion may take 10–15 years, plot owners remain dependent on the promoter/TMA indefinitely.
CATCH #8: Disconnection of Essential Utilities on Any Default Clause 12.9
The Legal Trap: Clause 12.9 allows the private TMA to unilaterally disconnect water, sewage, and electricity connections for non-payment of maintenance. In Maharashtra, shutting off essential water and sanitary services to residential occupants has been held illegal by the High Court and RERA Appellate Tribunals, yet the promoter has codified it into this agreement.
CATCH #9: Exclusion of Marketing Collateral & Oral Promises Clause 9.6.14 & Clause 23.1
The Legal Trap: Clause 9.6.14 states that the promoter has "withdrawn all its advertisements", and Clause 23.1 states that no oral commitments, sales brochures, or artistic renders have any legal validity. Any verbal assurance given by the sales team (e.g., regarding water delivery, bypass road access, or clubhouse completion) is legally null and void unless written into the agreement.
CATCH #10: Architectural Veto & Construction Timeline Pressure Clause 9.2 & Clause 9.5
The Legal Trap: You cannot construct freely on Plot R5-180. Your villa design, external elevation, exterior paint shades, and compound wall height must be vetted and approved by the promoter's architect. The promoter can demand adherence to strict aesthetic guidelines at your expense.
